# Ethics and Risk

## Responsible positioning

BotTradeNC is a research and software engineering project. It must not be marketed as a guaranteed profit system.

## What the project may claim

It may claim to demonstrate:

- market data capture,
- data quality validation,
- feature engineering,
- strategy simulation,
- backtesting methodology,
- AI-assisted analysis,
- operational reliability,
- documentation and reproducibility.

## What the project must not claim

It must not claim:

- guaranteed returns,
- risk-free trading,
- autonomous wealth generation,
- investment advice,
- verified profitability without transparent evidence.

## Human supervision

AI analysis should support decisions, not replace accountability. Live execution, if ever added, must include hard risk controls, logs, alerts and human oversight.
