# Portfolio Positioning

## One-line English positioning

BotTradeNC is an AI-native quantitative research system for crypto markets, focused on data quality, reproducible backtesting, explainable strategy analysis and human-supervised trading research.

## One-line Spanish positioning

BotTradeNC es un sistema AI-native de investigación cuantitativa para mercados cripto, enfocado en calidad de datos, backtesting reproducible, análisis explicable de estrategias y supervisión humana.

## What this project demonstrates

- Software architecture.
- Data engineering.
- Quantitative analysis.
- Backtesting methodology.
- Automation.
- Operational monitoring.
- AI-assisted analysis.
- Documentation discipline.
- Ethical product positioning.

## What not to say

Avoid:

- “This bot makes money automatically.”
- “Guaranteed profits.”
- “Passive income trading system.”
- “AI decides when to buy and sell.”

Prefer:

- “Research system.”
- “Backtesting lab.”
- “AI-assisted analysis.”
- “Human-supervised decision support.”
- “Portfolio-grade software project.”
